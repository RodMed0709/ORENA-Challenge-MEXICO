# External data: permitted, and what it costs us

## Why this note exists

Two sessions argued licences from summaries. The 2026-07-31 sweep
(legokna's licence sweep) reached the right verdict — `CC BY-NC-SA` is not a
disqualifier — but rested it on a reading of the `lapchole` DUA that the organizers themselves would
not confirm. Meanwhile the four sections of the challenge design that actually govern the question
had been sitting extracted in the repo, unread, cited only by an archived summary
(an archived vault note) that had already planted one falsified premise.

**Cite the PDF, not a paraphrase.** `ORena-FOCUS-challenge-design-FRAME-track.pdf` is tracked at the
repo root; page numbers below are its own.

## What the organizers answered (e-mail, 2026-08-03)

Asked three questions, they answered:

1. **"yes, training on public CC-BY-NC-SA or CC-BY-SA datasets is permitted for the challenge"**
2. On whether trained weights are a derivative work: *"this is a legal question that to the best of
   our knowledge has not been settled in court, the conservative viewpoint would be that the trained
   model weights are a derivative work, but we cannot give legal advice in this matter"*
3. **"yes, documenting all data sources in the method description is both necessary and sufficient
   in this case"**

🔻 **Answer 2 goes against the sweep's reasoning, not against its verdict.** The sweep held that the
`lapchole` DUA had already settled the derivative-work question. It has not: the organizers had that
same clause in front of them and still declined to rule. The DUA sentence — *"Algorithms and models
generated by individual teams may be used for noncommercial or commercial purposes"* (p.9, verbatim)
— governs **use of the model**, not whether the weights are a derivative work.

🔑 **The verdict survives by a shorter route.** Answers 1 and 3 are the rule of the challenge, from
the body that sets it. We no longer need to win a copyright argument.

## The four sections that bind (PDF, verbatim)

### §Training data policy (p.7)

> Participants may train models using the challenge-provided training set and may additionally use
> publicly available datasets and publicly released pre-trained models (e.g., open VLM backbones,
> language models, or vision encoders). Use of additional manually or automatically generated
> annotations based on public data is allowed provided that annotations are made publically
> available after the challenge. All training/fine-tuning data must be specified. Any external data
> or pre-trained model must be publicly accessible at the time of the launch of the pre-evaluation
> phase to ensure fairness and reproducibility. Participants may create additional annotations for
> such public datasets (including private annotations) and use them for training, provided that they
> make them publicly available with the submission of their solution.

Three obligations, none of them a licence test:

- **A date.** Publicly accessible by the pre-evaluation launch — **2026-07-15** (§Schedule, p.9).
  A dataset released after that is ineligible however permissive it is.
- **A disclosure.** *All* training/fine-tuning data specified in the method description. This is the
  same thing answer 3 calls necessary and sufficient.
- **A publication.** Annotations we generate on third-party public data ship publicly with the
  solution.

### §Award policy (p.7)

> The prize money will be distributed to the top three teams that meet the following criteria:
> 1) The team will make their model and any additionally created annotations public. 2) The
> submission outperforms the baselines. 3) Team members are eligible to receive prize money.
> 4) The submission description provides sufficient methodological details […]

🔑 **Criterion 1 says "public". It names no licence.** So even under the conservative reading — the
weights are a derivative work and third-party ShareAlike propagates — releasing them as `CC BY-NC-SA`
would satisfy the criterion. **ShareAlike inheritance is not a blocker for prizes or co-authorship.**
It constrains only which licence we may put on the release, never whether we may release.

Also fixed here: the baselines are **a frontier VLM applied zero-shot** and **an open-source VLM
fine-tuned by the organizers**, and per §Organizer policy (p.7) they *"will be clearly identified as
such on the leaderboard"* — closing the open question in `NOW.md` 2026-07-27.

### §Data usage agreement (p.9)

The DUA we signed forbids, in clause (3), *"to not publish the data or underlying annotations, or
otherwise make them publicly available"*.

🔑 **This does NOT collide with the publication duty above, because the two have disjoint scope:**

| annotations created on… | obligation |
|---|---|
| third-party **public** datasets | **must be published** with the submission (§Training data policy) |
| **challenge** data (`heico` / `lapchole`) | **must NOT be published** (DUA clause 3) |

Operationally: SAM 2 masks generated over our own videos are probe output and stay in the secure
environment; masks generated over a public corpus and used for training must be released.

Also recorded from the same page: the 30 HEICO videos are already public under `CC BY-NC-SA`, and
the training QA pairs will be released `CC BY` **except** the HEICO-derived ones, which follow
`CC BY-NC-SA`.

### §Publication policy (p.8)

> All teams that have beaten the baselines will be invited as co-authors on the planned publication.
> **Each team can name up to three co-authors.**

We are three. The Core Value's ceiling and our headcount match exactly.

## What this settles about datasets on the table

- **`CC BY-NC-SA` is not a disqualifier.** Confirmed by the organizers directly, not inferred.
  Supersedes the reasoning (not the verdict) of legokna's licence sweep.
- **SurgΣ-DB passes the date gate.** Its HF update note is dated **2026-04** and its 3.09 GB payload
  last changed ~4 months before 2026-08-03 — i.e. public roughly three months before the 2026-07-15
  cutoff. ⚠️ A commit timestamp shows when a file changed, not when the repo became public; if the
  dataset ever becomes load-bearing for a submission, settle it against the HF commit history or an
  archive snapshot before shipping.
- **CholecT50** (the images SurgΣ-DB does not bundle) has been public since 2021 — no date risk. It
  still has to be requested from CAMMA on their terms.
- **Anything discovered after 2026-07-15 and released after it is out**, regardless of quality.

## What is still NOT answered

Whether trained weights are a derivative work. The organizers decline, and no court has ruled. We
proceed because the challenge's own criteria do not depend on the answer — **not** because the
question is closed. Any claim outside this challenge (a commercial release, a different venue)
re-opens it.
