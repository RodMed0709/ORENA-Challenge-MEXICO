Worth ≈ **+0.023 headline** as a second answer channel — above the readable floor, **below the S1 bar**, so not a submission on its own. Also explains rung 33's gate-A failure: the information does not survive the projection into tokens, so a decoding fix was working on the wrong side of the break. `experiments/34-hidden-state-probe/`.
- [[moving-the-distribution-cannot-fix-off-by-one]] — 🔴 **the family is closed on a MECHANISM, not a tally.** NTL-WAS did exactly what it was built to do — bias **−0.420 → −0.342**, mean prediction 2.27 → 2.35, off-by-≥3 **14.3% → 13.3%**, emitted `"1"` 772 → 676 and `"4"` 204 → 267 — **and lost accuracy 0.4709 → 0.4489.** Of the **271** `number` questions A2 got right and NTL lost, **161 moved +1 against 87 that moved −1**: it pushed already-correct answers one step off. The off-by-one RATE did not budge, **65.8% → 65.7%**. 🔑 It is the *global +1 shift* (measured dead at 0.4718 → 0.2197) in soft, learned form — the bias is an **average, not an offset**. Five interventions have now failed and all five **move probability mass**: shift, LUT, k-voting, GRPO, NTL. Rung 33 said why in advance — when greedy is wrong the gold is the runner-up only **45.6%** of the time, so the near-ties are not one nudge away. **Off-by-one describes the residual, not a fixable displacement.** ⚠️ Does NOT close interventions that change WHICH questions are near-ties — [[hidden-states-hold-the-count]] is the only branch left with a measured basis. 📌 The veto damage was **gradient starvation, not leakage**: 0.00% of `fo_class` answers carry a digit in either arm, so the −0.088 is the pre-registered confound firing as written.
- [[segment-leaderboard-and-the-bar]] — 🎯 **the SEGMENT bar is 0.5118 and our backbone with a PLAIN fine-tune already touches it.** Official proprietary baseline 0.5118 (rank 14 of 64), official FT baseline 0.4927 (rank 19); clearing both — the co-authorship condition — means beating 0.5118, done by only 13 of 64. 🔑 Ranks 12 and 15 are `Fine tuned Qwen3 VL 8B` at **0.5155 / 0.5114** — our exact backbone straddling the bar within ±0.004, so everything above it is recipe. SEGMENT is worth **~40 %** of the prize vs FRAME's 20 %, is **video** VQA (5 groups, 10 buckets, 15 s/question on 80 GB, `time` = 38.2 %), and 🔴 **its trivial floor is enormous** — the headline is an unweighted mean over 10 buckets and several are tiny (`aggregation_ID` is n=23 over 21 templates), so you lose by breaking easy buckets, not by failing to lift hard ones.
- [[segment-arm-a-is-one-broken-bucket]] — 🟢🔴 **the arm nobody read scores 0.4964 against a 0.5118 bar, and the ENTIRE gap is one bucket answering `00:00:00`.** `temporal_grounding` (2,436 q, 39 % of the corpus, 2/10 of the headline) scores **0.0351 / 0.0457** against floors of 0.4392 / 0.3299 — **72.5 % of its raw outputs are literally `00:00:00`**, with **0 malformed predictions**, which rules out the harness and makes it [[zero-is-format-localized]]'s attractor on a new axis: the relative-time rewrite concentrated the target near zero. 🔑 **Lift that bucket to nothing better than its own floor and `bucket_mean` gains +0.0688 → 0.565, clear of the bar**, with every other bucket unchanged. 🟢 `object_recognition` (2,823 q) beats its floor on BOTH halves (+0.029 / +0.064) ⇒ the FRAME recipe transfers. Needed three things that did not exist: a SEGMENT `predict()`, the deleted A2 base reconstructed from its surviving adapter, and two harness fixes (`Track.FRAME` hardcoded ⇒ 5 s cap on a 15 s track, scoring ≈0 at rc=0; `normalize_answer` not repairing `hh:mm:ss.` which `Time.verify` RAISES on).
- [[debate-works-and-the-roles-are-backwards]] — 🟢🔴 **the critique is worth four points and we gave it to the WRONG MODEL.** Two VLMs both seeing the frame, three turns, 1,283 questions on rung 42's 8 held-out videos: `A_debate` beats its own `A_selfrevise` control by **+0.0497** on the pre-registered `ALL_ID` cell (CI [+0.0043, +0.0990]) and `A_alone` by **+0.0399 `bucket_mean`**, while **self-revision is FLAT (−0.0021)** — so the gain is the OTHER MODEL'S CRITIQUE, not a second look. Cleanest single-variable inference-time result of the campaign, and it clears `§S1`. Acts where designed: they disagree on 34.7 % and there it is worth **+0.11**. 🔴 **And it does not ship: the 8B ALONE scores 0.6727 against the pipeline's 0.6314** — the debate lifts the 27B four points and the lifted 27B still loses to the checkpoint we already have, at 1/5 the compute. 📌 Harness validated in passing: rung 42 reported 0.6744 for this checkpoint on these same questions, this path measures 0.6727 (the known ~0.5 % GPU-swap drift). ⇒ **next arm is one variable — SWAP THE ROLES**, 8B decides and the 27B critiques (its enumeration is 0.670 vs 0.317, so its critique is aimed at `number` = 71 % of our errors). ⚠️ 8 videos, **no OOD CI is readable** (one returned a ZERO-WIDTH interval — a 2-cluster bootstrap is an artefact); and 27B FP8 + 8B does not fit one L40S. Carries two side findings: our SFT'd 8B **cannot follow a new output protocol** (24/60 ignored it, emitting bare answers), and `G-FORMAT` caught the debate turn provoking `"2."`.
- [[trace-extractor-is-a-coin-flip]] — 🔴 **the "83 % of failed traces contain the gold" figure is an ORACLE RECALL WITHOUT ITS DENOMINATOR, and the two-model trace-extraction design dies on it.** Measured on rung 43's 4,000 archived generations (1,326 `number`, 963 failed, heico/OOD only), zero GPU: a median of **3** distinct plausible values coexist per failed trace (p90 6), only **82 of 963** hold exactly one, and **83.0 % also contain `gold ± 1`** — the extractor's job is not *find the number*, it is *decide between 2 and 3*, which is the original task with the picture removed ([[counting-has-two-failure-modes]] seen from inside the trace). The two trivial extractors score **0.1786** (last before `</think>`) and **0.2233** (most frequent) against a **0.2189** random-pick control — they ARE the chance line — and they **break 24.2 %/37.7 % of the questions the model already answered right**, which `RULES §S8` vetoes. End to end over all 1,326: extractor **0.337** vs the no-thinking control **0.481** (−0.144). 🔑 **The 83 % reproduces only under the LOOSEST extraction (0.8577), which is also the one with the MOST candidates (median 4)** — recall and ambiguity are one quantity read from two sides; strict reading gives 0.6781. ⇒ fourth independent confirmation of [[self-consistency-dead]]'s mechanism. 🟢 Does **not** touch [[hidden-states-hold-the-count]] — a text trace is a lossy function of the state the probe reads.
- [[counting-has-two-failure-modes]] — 🔑 **counting is TWO defects, not one, and the campaign has been averaging them.** On 2,094 scored `number` questions (rung 30's own evals, zero extra GPU): `gold <= 4` is **83% of the format**, is essentially **unbiased** (−0.125) and **77.8% of its errors are off by exactly ONE**; `gold >= 5` is 17%, bias **−1.841**, only 34.1% adjacent. Overall **65.6% of all errors are off-by-one**, off-by-≥3 is 14.4%. Ceiling if every near miss were converted: `number` **0.4718 → 0.8185** (an upper bound, not a forecast) — a third of that is ≈ **+0.045 headline**, clearing S8 and S1. ⚠️ Confirms [[counting-is-a-mapping-failure]] rather than contradicting it: its 0.487/≈−2 replicate at 0.448/−1.84 **on the `gold >= 5` tail**, which is where it was measured. **The proximity/soft-label family is ALIVE** — its own kill test ("if errors are off-by-≥2, exact-match scoring makes proximity worthless") was run and came back the other way. Still ungated: whether the number-token logits are ordinally structured (~1 GPU-h dump) — do not fund an arm before it.
- [[letterbox-crop-is-not-free-resolution]] — 🔻 **the "+27% free resolution" pitch was wrong in its mechanism, twice.** A rectangle cannot remove a **circular scope vignette** (`report.py:86-88` says so): of 36.4% dark on `heico` a crop reaches **18.3%**, of 26.3% on `lapchole` it reaches **10.5%** — ~half is unreachable corner. And on native-resolution Qwen3-VL with `max_pixels` **non-binding**, a crop buys **zero pixels-per-object** — it only drops tokens, and p99 0.352 s means there is no latency motive either. 📌 Cached frames are **960×540 / 720×576**, not the 1280×720 the config assumes. **Not dead** (both splits clear the 8% kill line) but demoted, and **ID/OOD-asymmetric 10.5% vs 18.3%** against a ranking that weights the halves equally. Settle with the 3-arm inference-only A/B (crop / crop+restore-grid / control) before any GPU.
- [[counting-is-a-mapping-failure]] — the model **SEES** the objects and cannot **EMIT** the number: Spearman 0.487 at gold ≥5 (0.661 full range), bias ≈ −2. Scalar low-count data does not transfer; point supervision does.
- [[cross-question-constraints]] — 🟢 **questions about the same frame constrain each other and we never used it**: 495 frames pair a per-class count with the total, 979 pair a `number` with a `fo_class`. Free, exact CoA ground truth already inside `train.parquet`. ⚠️ **TRAIN-time only** — at inference the SDK `Request` carries no sibling questions, so it must be distilled into the weights.
