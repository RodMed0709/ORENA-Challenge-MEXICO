## Why one document for three routes

CoA, plain CoT and CoVT were treated as competing alternatives for three sessions. They are not.
They share **one gate** (rung 17 — can the teacher see?), **one piece of engineering** (a loss
hook that did not exist anywhere in the repo), and **one fork** (rung 24 — is the vision tower
still teachable?). Ordering them is what makes them closeable.

## 1. The corpus is not anti-CoT — it is anti-**emitting**

The `[coa]` literature reads as uniformly negative. It is not: it splits on an axis nobody was
reporting — *does the trace get generated at inference, or only trained on?*

**Against emitting at inference, on perception tasks:**

| source | result |
|---|---|
| **v01** Chain-of-Adaptation — *our exact backbone* | scaffold in the SFT target, no RL: **62.0 vs 65.7** bare-gold (EndoVis2018). The +18 is RLVR's, and RLVR with **no** thinking tags already beats SFT (67.4 vs 65.7) |
| **v10** Surgery-R1 | the two SFT variants differ **~1 pt in opposite directions**; +RFT adds ~8 ID / ~10 cross-set |
| **v06** | CoT lowers accuracy ~3% over 13 spatial benchmarks; **7 of 8 distilled reasoning models fail to beat their own backbone** |
| **v07** | names **visual grounding and object counting** as what CoT degrades — our two weakest cells |
| **v40** | the mechanism: CoT induces **attention dispersion** |
| **our rung 23** | emitting a trace at `max_new_tokens 64` scores **0/24** — the trace truncates before the answer |

**For training on traces and not paying for them at inference:**

| source | result |
|---|---|
| **v18** STaR | **+12.5% over direct-answer SFT at the same data scale**, via correctness-filtered rationalization — which is exactly our reverse-generation design |
| **v47** 3TF | Qwen3-8B **93.0 vs NoThinking's 89.9 at the same 241 tokens**; its §4.6 ablation says reasoning-bearing targets beat answer-only targets |

🎯 **No paper trains once on a scaffold and evaluates the SAME checkpoint under both emit and
suppress on a perception benchmark.** That cell is empty and it is what rung 09 should test.
Consequence: **"CoA" and "plain CoT" are the same experiment** — the suppress arm.

⚠️ Both positive results are **text-only, on reasoning tasks** — precisely the category v07 says
CoT helps. The transfer to perception is the untested part and cannot be argued, only measured.

🔴 And per **v48**, a derived trace is a **target-side** change, the damaging kind (~21% vs
harmless for input-side). Its measured error rate must exist *before* training, not after.

## 2. CoVT: three blockers fall, two remain

Still **NO-GO as published** ([[covt-reduced-sam-route]]): a 774.6k-row corpus, 17K steps, and a
pipeline no recipe framework expresses (ms-swift, LLaMA-Factory and Unsloth alike). But re-reading
the supplementary (ficha §v46):

**Fall:** the vision experts run **only during training**, so nothing ships in the inference
container · all dense GT is **generated by those same experts**, so annotation cost is zero · the
only non-permissive expert (**PIDINet**, research-only) is also the one whose tokens move counting
by **−0.1**, so dropping it costs nothing.

**Remain:** the early training stages are **load-bearing** (stages 3&4 alone score *below* base),
and it needs a **trainer outside ms-swift**.

And its own ablation tightens the design: for counting, **segmentation is the only expert that
helps and DINO actively hurts** (65.0 → 66.0 seg-only; 64.7 DINO-only). So a **one-expert,
8-segmentation-token** reduction is better aimed at our target than the full method, cheaper, and
licence-clean.

🔑 Under our own noise rule, CoVT's risk profile is **better than CoA's**: its visual loss is
**auxiliary, on extra tokens, with the answer's CE untouched**, so noisy masks degrade the
auxiliary term rather than poisoning the answer gradient. In CoA the generated prose *is* the
target sequence carrying the answer.

Why 0.1 mattered: rung 24's pre-registered rule says *"a tower that is **indifferent** ⇒ the branch
is mapping"* and **"indifferent" had no numeric definition** — the same hole that lost rungs 14 and

### Phase 1 — the gate we never ran (needs pod)
**Rung 17.** The rung-09 generator receives the gold and writes reasoning that *derives* it, so a
perception failure **cannot appear as a wrong answer** — it appears as `<evidence>` describing a
scene that is not there, with a correct `<answer>` by construction, and the judge-mirror is a text
model that cannot see the frame either. Pre-registered: margin ID **and** OOD > 0 ⇒ GO; either
< 0 ⇒ **STOP**.

### Phase 2 — the loss hook (zero GPU) 🟡 STARTED
`compute_loss_func` existed nowhere in the repo. One hook, four uses:
- **2a SCALe** (v02) — weight `<answer>` against `<think>` on a cosine schedule. Without it a
  scaffold puts ~99% of the gradient on prose (70–120 trace tokens vs a ~2.4-token gold), which is
  [[coa-sft-published-null]]'s mechanism.
- **2b NTL** (v12) — auxiliary numeric-proximity loss, masked to `number`. 🔒 gated on the
  `number-probe`: a proximity loss needs a distribution to widen.
- **2c** — the auxiliary-loss shape phase 4 needs.
- **2d non-dilution** (v05) — per-format weights. ✅ built.

Two invariants, both from rung 22's measurements: **OFF is byte-identical** (the factory returns
`None`, so the trainer takes its ordinary branch), and **reweighting is mean-preserving** because
`max_grad_norm` is 1.0 and clipping is the one channel a loss rescale is not invariant to.

### Phase 3 — CoA/CoT done properly = the **suppress arm** (rung 09 stage 2)
One experiment. All conditions mandatory: short gold-anchored scaffold generated on-pod (DUA) ·
**STaR correctness filter** (free — we hold the gold) · **SCALe loss** (without it we reproduce the
published null at our own expense) · **matched 2k bare-gold control** · 🎯 **the same checkpoint
scored under emit AND suppress, paired** · p99 in suppress mode before the full run · F1cls
reported · 1–2 epochs, number-aware selection · booked as a **cold start for a later RLVR rung**,
not a standalone win. Honest prior: `bucket_mean` ≈ 0 (−0.02 to +0.02).

### Phase 4 — CoVT-lite
One expert (**SAM 2, 8 segmentation tokens**), expert-generated GT, auxiliary loss with the
answer's CE untouched, no expert in the Docker. The mask pipeline is **shared with the label-
correction route** ([[covt-reduced-sam-route]]) and with rung 25 — one pipeline, three deliverables.

🔴 **The risk, named before starting.** Localization supervision carries three negatives and one
+1.0:

| source | result |
|---|---|
| **v05** Point-Detect-Count, *our own model family* | counting-only **0.26** vs counting+pointing **1.52** — **5.8× worse** |
| **[[prompt-only-pointing-collapses]]** (ours) | the model emits exactly **1 point in 681** questions |
| **rung 15** (ours) | the label-supported half of point-then-count → **null** |
| **v46** CoVT, same backbone | seg-only counting 65.0 → **66.0 (+1.0)** |

v05 and v46 **contradict each other on the same model family**, and nothing of ours separates them.
Three reconciliation candidates, none measured: **auxiliary vs inline** (v05's points share the
output sequence with the count; CoVT's tokens do not — the favoured hypothesis), v05 is **not a
paired ablation** (n = 105/91/98/99), and **+1.0 does not separate "helps" from "does nothing"**.
⇒ Before building a bespoke trainer, the design must articulate why the auxiliary form escapes
v05's result. If it cannot, phase 4 is discarded here, for free.

## 5. What rung 26 found along the way

**40.6% of the 6,252 val questions hand the model part of the answer** — a stated cardinality
(*"There is one surgical foreign object visible…"*) or a named entity (*"How many **Clips**…"*) —
and it is **not** confounded with ID/OOD. The margin gap this buys is **heterogeneous by format**:
`fo_class` ~doubles (+0.22 in both distributions), `number` gains **nothing** (−0.02), and `binary`
OOD sits **below its trivial floor** (−0.1576 over 311 questions). 🔴 The pooled gap (+0.0493) is a
format-mix artefact — do not quote it. Full pre-registration in
`experiments/26-deshortcut-eval/PLAN.md`.
