<objective>
Build the ONE frame-sampling policy shared identically by eval, serve, and (later) Phase-3 training-export — the single real design decision of Phase 2 (DATA-04). A frozen `SamplingPolicy` (k, selection rule, max_pixels cap) plus a pure `select_indices` and two entry points — `sample_frames(clip_path)` for a temp MP4 and `sample_frames_from_paths(frame_paths)` for `FocusFrameDataset.frame_paths` — that route through the SAME selection + resize, so train and serve see identical pixels. Also lays the Wave-0 CPU-test scaffold (StubEngine, synthetic mp4 fixture, `make_video_sample`) that Plans 02-02/02-03 consume.

Purpose: A mismatch between the frames the model trains on and the frames it serves on silently tanks OOD accuracy (RESEARCH Anti-Patterns "different frame selection in training vs serve"). Pinning selection + the pixel cap in one imported module — and putting the cap INSIDE the sampler, never in `FocusVideoDataset` (the platform builds the clip on submission; we only receive `video_path`+`fps`) — is what keeps FRAME under the 5.0s latency gate and keeps every seam consistent.
Output: `src/frame/engine/{sampling,config}.py`, the extended `tests/conftest.py` scaffold, and `tests/test_sampling.py`.
</objective>
