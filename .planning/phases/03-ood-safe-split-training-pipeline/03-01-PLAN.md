<objective>
Build the frozen, leakage-proof, video-level train/val split (DATA-02) — the foundation every future training and eval number references. Partition strictly on `videoID` (never per-frame): all HeiCo videos become the OOD-val set, a seed-shuffled ~18 LapChole videos become the in-domain (ID) val set, the rest are train. Serialize to a committed JSON manifest carrying a SHA-256 hash over the three sorted videoID lists; assert zero pairwise `videoID` intersection; and provide a `load_verified` that re-hashes and refuses to run on an edited manifest. All CPU, deterministic, network-free in tests.

Purpose: Any `videoID` in two splits inflates val accuracy and the leaderboard exposes the illusion (the organizers' own CI resamples by video). A non-reproducible split makes the 3 machines' numbers non-comparable. The hashed manifest is the "referenced by every run" contract (ROADMAP Phase-3 SC1 + SC4; CONSTITUTION §IV.3/§IV.8; PITFALLS #1/#2).
Output: `src/frame/data/{split,manifest}.py`, `configs/split.yaml`, `scripts/make_split.py`, and CPU tests.
</objective>
