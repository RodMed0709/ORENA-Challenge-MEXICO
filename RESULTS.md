**Read `margin`, not raw accuracy.** `margin_ID`/`margin_OOD` = `acc − floor`, where the floor is the template-aware trivial constant (answer each template's modal answer; data card rung 08 §4b). It is the REAL skill the model adds. `acc_OOD > acc_ID` does NOT mean the model generalises better — the OOD floor is ~12 pts higher, so by margin the model usually adds *less* on OOD. A negative margin = below the trivial floor (a weak baseline legitimately is). Blank margins = `needs_backfill` (no gold answers joined yet).
