# Image Preprocessing / Enhancement for Laparoscopic & Endoscopic Imagery — Literature Sub-Corpus

Ranked, citable knowledge base for the **image-side** question in our ORena SAVE FOCUS
(FRAME track) entry: *can anything we do to the pixels — before, during or after training —
move `bucket_mean` on foreign-object VQA?*

## The question this corpus exists to answer

Our teammate ran an image-processing rung and produced four negatives: (1) unsharp mask at
**inference only** on an already-fine-tuned model cost −0.026 / −0.056 margin and was monotone
in dose; (2) a zero-GPU AUC-separability screen of 32 operators/pipelines (unsharp, CLAHE,
despecular, bilateral, homomorphic, dehaze, tophat, sobel, morph-gradient, and chains) validated
**nothing** after Bonferroni; (3) **training with** an edge map as a second image was null
(+0.021 fo_class ID, +0.0005 OOD, all CIs crossing zero); (4) the aux channel demonstrably
*reached* the model (16.2 % of answers changed) but moved accuracy at chance — so the bottleneck
was read as *discrimination, not access*. This corpus exists to check those four results against
what the literature actually does: **which operators, at which dose, at which point in the
pipeline, and — the axis we most suspect — whether anyone has evidence that pixel-side
enhancement helps a *learned* model (CNN / ViT / VLM) rather than a human viewer or a
hand-crafted descriptor.** Papers that show enhancement improving PSNR/SSIM but **not** a
downstream task metric are the highest-signal papers here and are flagged loudly.

## How this index is tiered

Papers are ranked by importance **to our setup** (single sampled frame, 960×540 / 1280×720 JPEG,
LoRA-tuned Qwen3-VL-8B, foreign-object VQA, `bucket_mean` diluted across 5 answer formats),
**not** by fame:

- **Tier 1 — directly actionable for our next experiment.** Papers that (a) measure a pixel-side
  intervention against a *downstream learned-model* metric, (b) settle the train/test-consistency
  axis our rung got wrong, or (c) give a concrete lever we can run on our own data next week.
- **Tier 2 — high value / method source.** The actual operator or architecture definitions we
  would implement if a Tier-1 paper says a family is worth trying: specular removal, desmoking,
  low-light, corruption benchmarks, resolution/tiling in VLMs, aux-modality injection.
- **Tier 3 — context.** Domain-generalization reviews, backbones, augmentation search, and
  restoration papers that are evaluated only perceptually — useful to cite, not to act on.

**Status column:** `open` / `arXiv` = open-access PDF downloaded to `pdfs/` and byte-verified
(`%PDF` header, non-trivial size). `paywalled — manual` = catalogued from abstract only; the
"Why it matters" text for those rows is **abstract-level only and marked as such**. No claim in
this index comes from a source we did not open or whose abstract we did not read directly.

Filenames follow `pXX_lastname_year_shorttitle.pdf`.

Tags in the "Why it matters" column: *[downstream-metric]* = evaluated against a task metric
(mAP/Dice/accuracy/F1), not PSNR/SSIM; *[perceptual-only]* = PSNR/SSIM/NIQE/human rating only;
*[train-test-axis]* = speaks to preprocessing consistency; *[negative]* = reports enhancement
failing to help or hurting a learned model.
