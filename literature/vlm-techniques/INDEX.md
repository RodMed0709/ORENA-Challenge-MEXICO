# VLM Techniques Sub-Corpus — What We Do *To The Model*

Ranked, citable sub-corpus for the **ORena SAVE FOCUS** challenge (MICCAI 2026, FRAME track).
Scope: **techniques applied directly to vision-language models** in laparoscopy, surgery and
medicine — adaptation (what to freeze, what rank, what LR), the **training target** (bare gold vs
reasoning scaffold), data construction, decoding, capability erosion during SFT, counting, OOD
generalization, and evaluation. Sibling corpus `literature/preprocessing/` owns everything that
happens to the *pixels* before the model; this corpus starts where that one stops.

## Why this corpus exists — the questions it must answer

Our measured state is a wall. Zero-shot Qwen3-VL-8B sits **below the trivial floor everywhere**
(bucket_mean 0.2557, margin −0.088 ID / −0.191 OOD). LoRA on the LLM lifts us to 0.5486; adding
the ViT gets 0.5667 — **and nothing has moved since**. Three failures define the agenda: (1)
**training erases counting** — `number` margin over floor OOD decays 1→3 epochs from +0.032 to
+0.004 (ViT frozen) and +0.015 to **+0.000** (ViT adapted), and checkpoint selection by `acc_OOD`
systematically picks the checkpoint that erased *more*; (2) **counting compresses toward the
prior** (bias −0.66, golds 3–4 share modal prediction 2, golds 5–8 share modal prediction 4) so
post-hoc calibration is provably dead; (3) **`fo_class` gets cardinality right and identity wrong**
— 1.24 classes emitted vs 1.25 gold, but `clip` is an attractor (875 emitted vs 615 gold) and
needle/bag/sponge are all confused *into* clip. Voting (k=16), post-hoc count calibration,
enumerate-then-count prompting, and inference-only input transforms are already measured dead.
Latency turned out to be pooled (p99 0.352 s against 120 s + B×5 s — ~14× headroom), so
token-hungry and multi-pass techniques are legal again. This corpus exists to tell us, honestly:
**does a CoA-format reasoning-scaffold SFT target generalize better OOD than the bare gold, without
RL — and should the scaffold be emitted at inference or suppressed?** Plus: what is actually known
about stopping SFT from eating a capability, and about making a VLM count.

## How this index is tiered

Tiered by importance **to our setup** (Qwen3-VL-8B + LoRA r=8/α=32/lr 2e-5, 13.7k questions, ~38
effective videos, bucket_mean over 4 equally-weighted cells read as margin over a template-aware
trivial floor, formats diluted, counting labels noisy at ±0.86 between frames ≤1 s apart) — not by
general fame.

- **Tier 1 — directly actionable for the next experiment.** Papers that change what we do in the
  next run: the CoA-scaffold line itself, the SFT-vs-RL generalization evidence, the
  capability-erosion mechanics, the counting-target work, and the surgical-VQA papers that
  measured our exact failure modes on our exact backbone.
- **Tier 2 — high value / method source.** Where a mechanism, a hyper-parameter, or a diagnostic
  actually comes from. Counting benchmarks that tell us *why* counting compresses; forgetting
  mitigations we could port; reasoning-target datasets.
- **Tier 3 — context.** Surgical benchmarks and technique papers we build on but do not need to
  innovate on; useful for the paper's related-work and for eval hygiene.

**Status** column: `arXiv`/`open` = open-access PDF verified and downloaded to `pdfs/`;
`ABSTRACT-ONLY` = ficha built from abstract/landing page only. Filenames follow
`vXX_lastname_year_shorttitle.pdf`. Every arXiv ID below was confirmed by downloading and
opening the file; nothing here is inferred.

Bracketed hints in "Why it matters": *[coa]* = bears on the CoA-format SFT decision,
*[erosion]* = capability loss during SFT, *[count]* = counting, *[peft]* = what to adapt and at
what LR, *[ood]* = ID/OOD gap, *[eval]* = measurement, *[data]* = data-side lever.
